Every bull on this page is drawn live in your browser by the same deterministic code that would run in the node build and on the site — seeded LCG, one skin colour driving a five-step ramp, an automatic outline pass. Nothing here is a mockup. Token #n renders identically everywhere, forever.

01

Common to legendary

Four real rolled tokens per tier, taken straight off the shuffle — no cherry-picking, so this is what each tier actually looks like. Three tells escalate together: the hide family, the horn colour (common is always plain bone; legendary rolls gilt or ivory), and boots (never on common, sometimes on uncommon, common above that). Armour contrasts the hide rather than tinting it — that single change is what separates a from livestock in a poncho. Legendary is gold because #F0B90B is BNB Chain's own colour.

02

A straight roll

Forty-eight consecutive token ids with no overrides — band, hide, eye, weapon and accessories all fall out of the seed. This is what a mint actually looks like. Click any bull to inspect its traits and its derived palette ramp.

03

The twelve weapons

The catalogue count is locked at twelve because the combat stats and matchup weights are keyed to it — these are sprite and name changes only, nothing in the sim moves. Each is held vertically through the fist so the grip always reads, and mirrors cleanly when a bull is flipped to face its opponent. The right horn tip reaches x 32, and the outline pass adds a pixel to both it and the weapon — so a single pixel of shape clearance still reads as a collision. The shaft sits at x 38 and no weapon head may extend left of SX−2, leaving two clear pixels between the outlines at the tightest point. That's a hard rule of the engine, not a per-weapon tweak; the reaper's blade sweeps outward for the same reason.

04

32×44 vs 48×56, actual size

A live fefer on the left, rendered by the fighting fefers generator itself; a bnbull on the right from this one. Same zoom, no scaling tricks. The fefer is a side-on silhouette with one readable feature — the jaw. The bull has to carry horns, a brow, a muzzle, armour with contrast trim and a held weapon, front-on. That is what the extra 16 × 12 pixels buy, and it is also why the pitchfork tines had to move: at 48 wide the horn sweep and the weapon column are close enough that a symmetric three-tine head only just fits.

06

Settled

Locked in as of this pass — recorded in the project docs.

  • Pricing: chainlink BNB/USD, with BNBULL always the discounted leg. Dollar stickers convert at pay time. The 10% discount is BNBULL and only BNBULL — implemented as an owner-settable discountBpsOf[asset] mapping under a hard MAX_DISCOUNT_BPS cap, so the rate is retunable without a redeploy.
  • Currency translation from fefers: $FEFER → $BNBULL, $USDT0 → $BNB. The rule for reading every line of the old codebase. Anywhere fefers bought or held $FEFER, bnbulls does the same for $BNBULL; anywhere it treated USDT0 as the native gas token, that's BNB — except BNB is volatile, so those sites need the oracle.
  • Socials, on every contract we deploy. x.com/WeAreBNBulls · t.me/WeAreBNBullsBulls Owner-settable strings plus @custom: natspec in each header. Not on the BNBULL token itself — four.meme owns that one, so it goes in bscscan token info instead.
  • Weapon clearance rule. Shaft at x 38, nothing left of SX−2 — two clear pixels between outlines. Fixed the horn tangle across all twelve.
  • The outline pass is 8-connected, runs twice, and the grid edge is off limits. Three separate holes in the silhouette, all now closed. Checking only the four orthogonal neighbours left every 45° edge unoutlined; running the pass once left accessories bare; and anything drawn on column 0 or 47 had nowhere to put an outline, which is what kept the horn tip raw. The composition is inset by a pixel and the build now sweeps all 3,960 band × hide × weapon × accessory × horn combinations, failing if any pixel lands on the border ring.
  • Onyx shades are inverted. Default dark-framed shades vanish on a near-black hide, so the onyx band gets a pale steel frame with a dark glint. Same object, flipped for contrast — the accessory palette is per-band already, so this needed no new mechanism.
07

Still yours to call

Four defaults I picked that you can still overrule.

  • Canvas size — 48×56, up from fefers' 32×44. The horns, armour and a readable angry brow don't fit in 32×44. Bigger canvas costs nothing on-chain (art is rendered off-chain from stats) but it is more art authoring. Say the word and I'll take it back down.
  • Gold as the top band, not blue. On fefers blue was the flex. Here the equivalent is BNB gold — it ties the rarest bulls to the chain's own brand colour. Blue means nothing on BNB.
  • Band names: dust / ember / venom / onyx / gold. Straight retheme of dusty / burnt / weird / golden / blue. Counts and clean-chances are carried over 1:1 so the rarity maths is unchanged.
  • No ears. At this size they land on the same rows as the eyes and turn the skull into a mushroom. The horns carry the silhouette. Reversible if you hate it.
